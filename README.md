# My Favorite Series: Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug and Cat Noir
---
## About the Series
*Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug and Cat Noir* is a **French animated superhero series** created by **Thomas Astruc**.  
It follows two teenagers, *Marinette Dupain-Cheng* and *Adrien Agreste*, who transform into **Ladybug** and **Cat Noir** to protect Paris from evil villains.
---
> "I'm not a regular girl, I'm a superhero!" — Ladybug
---
## Main Characters in the Series
1. **Marinette Dupain-Cheng / Ladybug**
2. **Adrien Agreste / Cat Noir**
3. **Tikki (Ladybug’s Kwami)**
4. **Plagg (Cat Noir’s Kwami)**
5. **Hawk Moth (Main Villain)**
---
## Reason why I love the Series
- The story is full of *adventure* and *romance*.  
- The animation is colorful and set in beautiful Paris.
- The characters are cute.
